Elizabeth Buckley has been praised by critics for possessing a voice of unique warmth and clarity. She is the recipient of the Presser Foundation Award for Outstanding Musician and the Grace Wilson Excellence in Singing Award. She was the winner of the Gunsalus Singing Competition, the 21st Century Piano Commission Competition, and the Kate Neal Kinley Memorial Fellowship. Ms. Buckley was a Regional Finalist in the New York Metropolitan Opera National Council auditions and a finalist in the Patrick G. and Shirley W. Ryan Opera Center at Lyric Opera of Chicago. She has performed the roles of Rosina in Il barbiere di Siviglia, Cherubino in Le nozze di Figaro, Mother Marie in Dialogues des Carmélites, Dido in Dido and Aeneas, Zita in Gianni Schicchi, Nancy in Albert Herring, Mrs. Splinters in Tender Land, Dafne in L’Euridice, Third Lady and Third Spirit in Die Zauberflöte, Iolanthe in Iolanthe, Phoebe Meryll in Yeomen of the Guard, Nettie and Julie Jordan in Carousel, Irene Molloy in Hello, Dolly!, and Sandy in Grease. In addition, she has an extensive background in recital and oratorio repertoire with an emphasis in Baroque performance practice.

From her background as a pianist, organist, violinist, and choral conductor, Elizabeth brings a great sense of musicality and sensitivity to her singing. She attended the Zoltán Kodály Pedagogical Institute of Music in Kecskemét, Hungary and holds a Bachelor of Music degree in vocal performance from Capital University, a Master of Music degree in vocal performance from the University of Illinois, and a Doctorate of Musical Arts in Music with a concentration in Performance and Literature from the University of Illinois in Urbana-Champaign. She toured with John Wustman in his Schubert Lieder Recital Series, and has collaborated with numerous musicians and ensembles in recital, oratorio, opera, musical theater, choral performances, and newly commissioned works. Elizabeth has performed as a soloist with Sinfonia da Camera, Champaign-Urbana Symphony Orchestra, Prairie Ensemble, Bach Collegium, Baroque Artists of Champaign-Urbana, the Chorale, Nova Singers, the Corelli Quartet, Concerto Urbano, the University of Illinois Symphony Orchestra, the University of Illinois Oratorio Society, the University of Illinois Chorale and Concert Choir, Danville Symphony, and Galesburg Community Chorus.

Currently, Elizabeth is serving as the Director of Music at St. Matthew's Lutheran Church in Urbana, Illinois. She has served as a visiting lecturer in voice at the University of Illinois and was Director of Music at Good Shepherd Lutheran Church in Champaign, Illinois. Elizabeth is preparing her dissertation on the historical context and performance practice of the sacred cantatas of Élisabeth-Claude Jacquet de La Guerre for publication, and will be performing as soloist in Vivaldi's Gloria with the Chorale and Clérambault's Leçons de Ténèbres with the Baroque Artists of Champaign-Urbana. Recent engagements include a performance of René Clausen’s A New Creation with the composer at the podium, and a recital featuring the sacred vocal cantatas of Jacquet de La Guerre.
